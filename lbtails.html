What is Leafborough?

The town and story of Leafborough is inspired by endless whimsical tails of woodland creatures and their coming of age experiences no matter what age they are coming into. The characters, setting, and stories are all original ideas and materials by Henry Wagner and Isabelle Rashkin.

The story follows The Ball Rabbit Family, Susan Duck Family, the Mayor and his wife, the Innkeeper and the Bartender, the landlords of the town, and more! All of them individually navigate their relationships and socio economic status in this quiet turn of the century city.

Leafborough is more obsessed with the innovation of the world around them than focused on the application and acceleration of any specific technology.

"Everyday is a lovely day with ones you love in Leafborough."
