About my original art...haha

For me and my visual art, it is all about having fun. All of my digital art is made through Procreate with an ipad pro, but I also enjoy physical media aswell.Some of my art ends up on my albums, but most of it is just for the pure joy of creating.

This page is my little corner to share my favorite hobby with you. I'm mostly just messing around with the intention of not letting it be too serious. Just my kinda freaky pieces that I love making. Hopefully they bring a little fun and inspiration your way too!
